## Example concise profile

```text
Profile name: housing-policy-skill-mismatch
Core topics: affordable housing policy, housing vouchers, spatial mismatch, skill mismatch, labor mobility, urban productivity
Secondary topics: place-based policy, housing subsidies, inter-city migration, human capital externalities
Study systems or organisms: cities, metropolitan areas, urban labor markets
Ecosystems or regions: United States, China, Europe
Methods or data types: causal inference (RDD, DID, IV), administrative data, census/ACS micro-data, spatial econometrics
Pollutants, stressors, or focal variables: not applicable
Exclusion topics: pure corporate finance, clinical health studies, purely theoretical models without empirical content
Must-track journals: Journal of Urban Economics, Journal of Public Economics, American Economic Journal: Economic Policy
Nice-to-track journals: Journal of Regional Science, Land Economics, Journal of Development Economics
Weekly reading capacity: 6 papers
Reminder threshold in days: 10
Preferred summary language: English or Chinese
Zotero collection or library target: Housing Policy
Obsidian vault path: ~/Documents/marginal-notes
Obsidian folder: Literature/Housing-Policy
High-relevance cues: causal identification of housing policy effects on labor mobility, explicit skill mismatch measurement, inter-city spillovers from housing interventions
Medium-relevance cues: transferable methods, adjacent policy contexts, international comparisons
Low-relevance cues: generic urban economics without housing or skill mismatch focus
Boundary concepts to track separately: general labor market search without spatial dimension, real estate finance without policy component
Concept confusion red flags: housing price effects treated as housing policy effects, spatial mismatch conflated with education mismatch, correlation of housing and wages presented as causal without identification strategy
```

## Example specialized profile: fiscal decentralization and local public goods

```text
Profile name: fiscal-decentralization-public-goods
Core topics: fiscal decentralization, inter-jurisdictional competition, public goods provision, tax competition, local government efficiency
Secondary topics: political economy of public finance, infrastructure investment, local government debt, spillovers across jurisdictions
Study systems or organisms: city governments, provincial governments, local authorities
Ecosystems or regions: China, United States, OECD countries
Methods or data types: panel data, spatial regression, instrumental variables, difference-in-differences, natural experiments
Pollutants, stressors, or focal variables: not applicable
Exclusion topics: purely theoretical public finance without empirical identification, clinical health economics
Must-track journals: Journal of Public Economics, Journal of Urban Economics, Journal of Comparative Economics
Nice-to-track journals: European Economic Review, Public Administration Review, Government Information Quarterly
Weekly reading capacity: 5 papers
Reminder threshold in days: 10
Preferred summary language: English or Chinese
Zotero collection or library target: Fiscal Policy
Obsidian vault path: ~/Documents/marginal-notes
Obsidian folder: Literature/Fiscal-Policy
High-relevance cues: explicit causal identification of fiscal policy effects, inter-city or inter-province spillovers, policy variation with clean identification strategy
Medium-relevance cues: international comparisons, historical natural experiments, transferable spatial methods
Low-relevance cues: pure theoretical models, macro models without micro-foundations
Boundary concepts to track separately: monetary policy, general equilibrium without jurisdiction dimension
Concept confusion red flags: fiscal competition conflated with political competition, public goods provision conflated with public investment returns, tax competition presented without welfare analysis
```
