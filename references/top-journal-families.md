# Top Journal Families — Public Economics, Urban Economics & Housing

Use this reference to create a watchlist for journals in: public economics, fiscal policy, urban economics, housing economics, regional science, labor economics, development economics, and related policy fields. Adapted from the generic ecology/environment seed pool to reflect the research profile of Fan Fan (范翻, CUFE).

Verified: 2026-05-12 against `journal_list.json` (742 journals, cufe+uibe ranks).

## Source priority

1. Official publisher journal pages and RSS/current-issue pages.
2. Crossref and OpenAlex source metadata for ISSN, publisher, and recent works.
3. CAS/JCR exports only for zone, quartile, and impact metadata.

Do not infer CAS zone or JCR quartile from journal prestige. If ranking data is missing, label `ranking pending`.

## Pool labels (adapted for economics)

- `flagship-general-full-track`: top-5 economics journals; track all items, filter by relevance.
- `field-top-full-track`: AAA/AA field-leading journals in public economics, urban economics, housing, labor, development; track all items.
- `field-relevant-only`: A/BBB field journals; include only papers with high or medium fit to the user's profile.
- `chinese-field-full-track`: high-ranking Chinese economics and management journals; track all items.
- `chinese-field-relevant-only`: Chinese journals in relevant areas; include high/medium relevance only.
- `working-paper`: NBER working papers; full track.

## Scope and tracking rules

Default scope: journals in or adjacent to **public economics, urban economics, housing economics, labor economics, development economics, fiscal policy, and social policy**.

Tracking priority order:
1. `flagship-general-full-track` — always scan, filter by relevance.
2. `field-top-full-track` — always scan, filter by relevance.
3. `field-relevant-only` — scan, include only high/medium relevance.
4. `chinese-field-full-track` — scan, filter by relevance.
5. `chinese-field-relevant-only` — include only high/medium relevance.
6. `working-paper` — NBER full track for working paper discoveries.

Rank basis: cufe rank (对外经贸大学) and uibe rank (中央财经大学) from `journal_list.json`. Label as `cufe:AAA / uibe:A` format in outputs.

When both cufe and uibe ranks are available, use the higher (lower numeric rank) of the two as the primary rank basis.
